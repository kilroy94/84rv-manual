Thermostat Operation

1
To turn the unit ON or OFF, press MODE.
2
For Air Conditioning, press Mode until you see Cool. Then press Fan until you see Auto.
3
For Heat, press Mode until you see Furnace.
4
Set the desired temperature. It may take up to three minutes for warm or cool air to begin blowing.
FACT
The Heat Pump option is not sufficient to stay warm. Use the furnace.
TIP
When set to Cool, air comes from the top vents. When set to Furnace, air blows from the bottom vents.

A/C & Troubleshooting

FACT
Your Air Conditioner will not operate unless you are plugged into electricity or have your generator running.
TIP
If your microwave lights are ON, you are either plugged into electricity or have the generator running.
1
Plug into shore power, or run the generator.
2
Inside the RV, set the thermostat to Cool and choose the desired temperature.

IMPORTANT

If you smell gas and hear a loud, rapid alarm, and you are sure the stove is OFF:

  1. Extinguish all flames.
  2. Do not touch electrical switches.
  3. Shut off propane at the tank supply.
  4. Open doors, windows, and roof vents.
  5. Leave the vehicle until the odor clears.
  6. Have the system checked before using it again.
